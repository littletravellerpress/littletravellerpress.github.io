01

The story

The night was quiet, the forest deep, and the Little Traveller walked alone, looking for home. Then a tiny light flickered in the air — a firefly, dancing in the dark, asking the only question that mattered: are you lost?

With a firefly to light the way and an owl to offer the kind of wisdom only owls have, the Little Traveller learns that being lost and being far from home are not the same thing — and that every journey is already part of the story.

"The path is never as dark as it seems."

"Home is not a place. It is the warmth you carry inside."

"Every journey is part of your story. And you are already home."
